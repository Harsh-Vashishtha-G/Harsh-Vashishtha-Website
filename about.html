About me

My Story

I started Haqikos in 2024 with a simple belief — India doesn't just need to use AI tools built elsewhere, it needs to build its own. What began as an idea inspired by JARVIS has grown into a working set of AI products: QIA, Gearist, and PayQIA — each solving a real problem for Indian users.

I'm currently completing my MCA with a specialization in AI & ML at Lovely Professional University, while building Haqikos in parallel. Most of what I know about shipping real AI products, I learned by actually building them — not just studying theory.

Outside of code, I create content on personality development through my YouTube channel, Identity Reborn, because I believe building skills and building mindset go hand in hand. Whether it's a line of code or a line of a script, I care about the same thing — making something genuinely useful.
